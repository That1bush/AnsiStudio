Community Rules

Effective date: 12 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-12-v1

These rules apply to everyone using ANSI Studio and its account features.

1. Respect other users

  • Do not harass, threaten, bully, or target people.
  • Do not impersonate another person or misrepresent account ownership.
  • Do not use the service to create or distribute unlawful or abusive material.

2. Keep accounts secure

  • Never attempt to access another user's account.
  • Do not share passwords or session details.
  • Do not probe, exploit, overload, or interfere with the website or database.

3. Use the tool fairly

  • Do not spam account creation or automated requests.
  • Do not bypass restrictions or abuse bugs.
  • Only upload images you have permission to use, and do not upload sensitive personal information.

4. Enforcement

We may investigate credible reports and restrict accounts or access when reasonably necessary to protect users or the service. Serious or repeated violations may result in suspension. These rules do not replace applicable law.

5. Report a problem

Before public launch, the site operator should add a monitored contact method for reporting security problems, abuse, and account issues.
