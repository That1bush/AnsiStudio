Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-12-v1

This policy describes the information the ANSI Studio starter application is designed to process. The site operator must check that this matches the deployed service and applicable privacy laws before launch.

1. Information we store

  • Account details: username, an optional email address, optional display name and profile picture.
  • Security data: a salted password hash (not your plain-text password), session-token hashes, session expiry, and the date/version of the terms you accepted.
  • Saved creations: the title, text, generated ANSI code, style, and creation timestamps for items you choose to save.
  • Settings: preferences such as style, formatting, site name and optional uploaded logo.
  • Technical data: Cloudflare may process connection and security information as part of hosting and protecting the site.

2. Why information is used

Information is used to create and authenticate accounts, keep sessions secure, store and display your saved creations and preferences, prevent abuse, maintain the service, and respond to legitimate support or legal requests.

3. Optional email and account recovery

Email is optional. The current starter implementation does not include an automated password-reset or email-verification flow. If you forget your password, you may lose access permanently; adding an email does not itself guarantee recovery. Do not enter an email address unless you understand it will be stored with your account.

4. Where data is stored

Account data is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database attached to the deployed Cloudflare Pages Functions. Cloudflare may process data in accordance with its own service terms and privacy documentation. Confirm your Cloudflare account, database region options, and applicable data-transfer requirements before launch.

5. Cookies and sessions

The account system uses a secure, HttpOnly session cookie to keep you logged in. The cookie is not intended for advertising and is not accessible to ordinary page JavaScript. Logging out invalidates the current session.

6. Sharing

The starter application does not provide public sharing of saved creations. We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by the hosting provider or disclosed when legally required, or when reasonably necessary to protect the service and its users.

7. Security and retention

Passwords are hashed before storage and session tokens are stored as hashes. No online service can promise perfect security. Account records and saved creations remain stored until removed by a supported feature or by the site operator under a documented retention process. The starter version does not yet provide a self-service account deletion button, so implement and document a reliable deletion/contact process before public launch.

8. Your choices

You can choose not to create an account, leave email blank, and avoid saving sensitive content. Before public launch, the site operator should publish a working contact method for access, correction, deletion and privacy questions, and provide any notices or consent required by applicable law.

9. Children and applicable law

Set an appropriate minimum age and supervision policy for your intended audience before launch. This policy does not replace obligations under applicable privacy and consumer law, including Australian law where it applies.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the service or its data practices change. If changes are material, users should be notified and any required consent obtained.

This is a starter policy, not legal advice. Ensure it accurately reflects the live service before launch.
